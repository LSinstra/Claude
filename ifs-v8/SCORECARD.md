## Verdict

**Do not book the audit.** The constraint is not document quality — several documents here are
better than what many certified sites hold. The constraint is **elapsed time with records being
kept**. KO 2, 6, 8 and 10 all need a demonstrable run of real records before a CB arrives, and
that run cannot be compressed or back-filled.

The documentation was largely rebuilt between 20 July and 11 August 2026. An auditor sees creation
dates. A system whose foundational risk analyses are all a fortnight old gets tested for
implementation, not accepted on content.
