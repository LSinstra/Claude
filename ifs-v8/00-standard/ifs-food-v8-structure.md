## The ten KO requirements

A KO scored D subtracts **50% of the total possible score** and the certificate cannot be issued;
an existing certificate is withdrawn from the IFS database within two working days.

| KO | Clause | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1.2.1`★ | Senior management ensures employees are aware of their responsibilities; mechanisms to monitor effectiveness, identified and documented |
| 2 | `2.3.9.1`★ | Monitoring system for each CCP — method, frequency, recording; each CCP under control, demonstrated by records |
| 3 | `3.2.2`★ | Personal hygiene requirements understood and applied by all relevant personnel |
| 4 | `4.1.3`★ | Customer agreements — compliance with agreed requirements |
| 5 | `4.2.1.3`★ | Raw material specifications (ingredients, additives, packaging, **rework**), up to date, unambiguous, legally compliant |
| 6 | `4.12.1`★ | Foreign material risk mitigation — risk-based procedures; contaminated product treated as non-conforming |
| 7 | `4.18.1`★ | Traceability — product lots linked to raw material and food-contact packaging batches |
| 8 | `5.1.1`★ | Internal audit programme covering **all** IFS requirements, within 12 months (execution ≤15 months), risk-based frequency, incl. off-site storage |
| 9 | `5.9.1`★ | Procedure for recalls, withdrawals, incidents and emergencies |
| 10 | `5.11.3`★ | Corrective actions formulated, documented and implemented, with responsibilities and timescales |

**N/A is not available** for KO 1, 3, and 5–10. Only KO 2 and 4 can ever be scored N/A.

## Scoring

| Result | Points | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | 20 | Full compliance |
| B | 15 | Almost full compliance |
| C | 5 | Part of the requirement is not implemented |
| D | −20 | The requirement is not implemented |
| Major | 0 | Substantial failure / process out of control / legal breach. **Subtracts 15%** of the total possible |
| KO at D | −20 | **Subtracts 50%** of the total possible |
| N/A | — | Excluded from the calculation; auditor must explain |

**KO scoring is a separate scale: A (20), KO-B (0 points), or D. A KO cannot be scored C.**
This is the single most consequential rule for Juicy Marbles right now: a KO that cannot be
positively demonstrated does not land softly on a C — it resolves to D.

Total score = points awarded ÷ ((232 − N/A count) × 20).

## Certification outcomes

| Outcome | Condition |
|---|---|
| Higher level | ≥ 95% |
| Foundation level | ≥ 75% and < 95% |
| Follow-up audit possible | Exactly one Major **and** ≥ 75% (certificate can then only be Foundation, even if ≥95%) |
| **No certificate** | More than one Major, **or** score < 75%, **or** any KO scored D |

Auditor must explain in the report: all ★ requirements (even when scored A), everything scored
B/C/D, every Major/KO, and every N/A.

## After the audit

- Action plan issued by the CB within **2 weeks** of the last audit day.
- **Corrections**: provided and implemented within **4 weeks**.
- **Corrective actions**: provided within 4 weeks, may be implemented later.
- An audit stopped before the checklist is completed counts as **cancelled**: current certificate
  withdrawn, no new certificate, new initial audit only after 6 weeks minimum.
