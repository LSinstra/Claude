## Doctrine v4 (July 2025) — what actually touches this audit

The doctrine is mostly about certification bodies and auditors. **Only four clarifications touch
Part 2 requirements**, and only two of them matter here:

| Doctrine item | Requirement | Relevance to Juicy Marbles |
|---|---|---|
| Allergen labelling clarification | `4.19.3` | ⚠️ **Relevant.** For adventitious or technically unavoidable presence, labelling of allergens and traces **shall be risk-based**, and must consider *both* declarable allergens processed on site *and* unintentional allergens arriving in raw materials. OBR32's quantitative carry-through calculation is exactly the right shape of evidence — make sure it covers the raw-material side too |
| Food defence applicability | `4.21.2` | ⚠️ **Relevant.** The external-inspection part of 4.21.2 is **not applicable** where no national food defence legislation requires such inspections *and* the company does not export to the US requiring FDA food defence inspection. Juicy Marbles markets in the US under the parent brand — **confirm the export/FDA position before claiming N/A**, and be ready to state it in the opening meeting |
| Procurement of animals/fish/seafood | `4.4.1` | Not applicable — plant-based |
| Traceability at slaughtering sites | `4.18.1` | Not applicable |

Also worth knowing from the doctrine, Part 1:

- **An audit stopped before the checklist is completed counts as cancelled** — certificate
  withdrawn, no new certificate, and a new initial audit only after six weeks minimum.
- The audit is carried out in the language of the production site. Slovenian is not on the
  no-interpreter list, so an interpreter is permissible under conditions (+20% audit duration).
- Unannounced audit registration is deactivated if nothing is uploaded within three months of the
  last possible audit day.
