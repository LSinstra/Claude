## Why the three "AT RISK" ones matter most

KO 1, 4 and 5 are close enough to recover with document work, and each one recovered removes a
50% deduction. KO 1 is roughly a day. The five confirmed D's are different in kind: they need
**records that accumulate over time**, and back-filling them retrospectively produces a far worse
finding than the gap itself — record falsification ends an audit.
